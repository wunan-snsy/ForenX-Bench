# ForenX-Bench v1.0.0: Experiment Summary

## Scope

Frozen TruFor and MVSS-Net inference on Columbia Uncompressed, CASIA v1 corrected, and COVERAGE corrected. Image-level threshold is fixed at 0.5. Localization results use only samples with valid, dimension-aligned reference masks.

## CASIA v1 independent replay

A seeded, stratified sample of 30 authentic, 30 copy-move, and 30 splicing images was replayed independently for each frozen model (90 attempts per model; seed 20260929). All sampled source hashes matched local manifests. At score and localization-map absolute-error tolerances of 1e-5, both TruFor and MVSS-Net reproduced all 90 sampled outputs (RSR=90/90; EGA=90/90 per model; Wilson 95% interval 0.959–1.000). Replay ran in a separate process on the same host and software environment; this does not establish cross-machine equivalence. Sample identifiers and replay records are omitted from the public release because permission to publish them was not verified.

## Data-quality disclosure

COVERAGE contributes all 200 images to detection. Nine tampered images with author-supplied masks whose dimensions do not match their images are excluded from localization metrics; therefore COVERAGE localization uses 91 samples.

## Reproduction and evidence

The repository includes code, configurations, aggregate results, and local audit procedures. Original inputs, model weights, per-file manifests and hashes, and sample-level evidence/replay records are excluded. Obtain data and model assets separately under their providers' current terms, generate manifests locally, and follow the documented run protocol.
